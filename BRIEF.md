# Home page brief (scroll-craft)

SUPERSEDED 2026-10-25 (round 3, Cam): the home page is now a standard shop home with no scroll moments; see DESIGN.md. Kept as history.

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation (Cam, 23 Oct 2026: "You write it, I review"). Built from Cam's answers: warm and crafted, light by default, drawn in code, home page only, no video, no paid generation. Cam reviews this with the home page preview and can change anything.

## The eight topics
1. **Vibe:** a quiet specialty roaster on a Saturday morning; printed, unhurried, precise. References: a well-made coffee label, a technical drawing of a brewer.
2. **Journey:** the shop and its idea first; how good coffee is made; what there is to buy; what this demo is.
3. **Energy:** calm open, one held moment in the middle, calm close.
4. **Feeling and the one moment:** curiosity, then absorption, then appetite, then trust. The moment: the pour-over drawing itself line by line under the visitor's own scroll.
5. **Something no site they have seen does:** the brewing steps are told by the drawing being made, not by photos or icons.
6. **Range:** premium-minimal leaning editorial (calm premium, per Cam's taste check).
7. **One world or scenes:** distinct scenes, short page.
8. **Assets:** none; everything drawn in code.

## Feeling curve
```
1  Curiosity    a calm hero: one sentence about finding coffee by taste, a layered line drawing with gentle depth
2  Absorption   the pour-over draws itself as the visitor scrolls; three real brewing steps arrive in order (the peak)
3  Appetite     the three ranges laid out unevenly, coffee largest, each with its own drawing
4  Trust        plain words on what this demo is and is not; one way on, into the shop
```

## The peak
"It's the site where the coffee brewer draws itself while you scroll." Act 2, the largest span on the page (about two and a half screens on desktop), preceded by the quiet hero.

## Tell-someone sentence
It's the site where you scroll and a pour-over gets drawn in front of you, step by step.

## Structure
- Grammar: chaptered editorial (short acts, a held middle). Filmic one-shot and continuous world lose because the shop needs a short home page that hands over to the catalogue; rhythmic cutlist is too loud for calm premium; live surface, typographic poster, gallery and split stage do not fit a story of four beats.
- Signature move: scroll-drawn line art (`stroke-dashoffset` on a view timeline), with step captions keyed to the same timeline.
- Fingerprint registry: none exists on this machine (the Desktop scroll-craft workspace is missing), so this is the first recorded build. The scroll-craft scripts were not run (their files changed after the 29 Sep audit and were not needed); screenshots and checks use this project's own Playwright scripts.
- Authored silence: none. Reduced motion shows the finished drawing and all three steps as a list.
